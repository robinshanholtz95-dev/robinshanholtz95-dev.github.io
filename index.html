About

I am a counselor-in-training in Northwestern University’s M.A. program in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and a research assistant in two labs, studying LGBTQ+ wellbeing and the psychology of reading.

My clinical and research work centers on affirming care for transgender and queer clients. I study how chosen family and online peer communities support queer people, and I am building a counseling group for transgender clients at my practicum site. At NYU’s Center for Conflict and Cooperation, I help run experiments and a meta-analysis on whether reading books changes prosocial behavior and polarization.

Before counseling, I spent eight years as a software engineer at Meta, leading privacy products used by Facebook’s users. I bring that background to research through programming, open and reproducible workflows, and R-based analysis. I am applying to PhD programs to continue this work.

  • Chosen family and the queer family life cycle
  • Online peer support communities
